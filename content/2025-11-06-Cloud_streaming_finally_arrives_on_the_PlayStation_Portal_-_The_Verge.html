PlayStation Portal Unleashes Cloud Streaming: A Game Changer!

PlayStation Portal owners can finally rejoice! According to The Verge, Sony's dedicated handheld is levelling up, now supporting cloud streaming. This highly anticipated update liberates players, allowing PS Plus Premium subscribers to stream a vast library of PS5 games directly from the cloud, without needing their personal console powered on or nearby.

Previously limited to Remote Play from a local PS5, this upgrade significantly boosts the PlayStation Portal's versatility and appeal. Now, enjoy your favorite titles from virtually anywhere with a stable internet connection. This marks a pivotal moment for portable gaming, transforming the PS Portal into a true cloud-enabled device and expanding the horizons for PlayStation gamers everywhere.
