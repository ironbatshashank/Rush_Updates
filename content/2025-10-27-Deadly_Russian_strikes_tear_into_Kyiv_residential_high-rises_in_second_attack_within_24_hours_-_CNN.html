Deadly Russian Strikes Hit Kyiv High-Rises in Second Attack Within 24 Hours

Kyiv has once again been rocked by deadly Russian strikes, with missiles tearing into residential high-rises across the Ukrainian capital. This marks the second devastating attack on Kyiv in less than 24 hours, according to reports including CNN.

The intense bombardment inflicted significant casualties and caused extensive damage to civilian infrastructure, leaving residents reeling. Emergency services are currently on-site, sifting through the debris of homes. These relentless Kyiv attacks underscore the brutal nature of the ongoing Russia-Ukraine war, with residential areas increasingly bearing the brunt of the conflict, deepening the humanitarian crisis and raising grave concerns for civilian safety.
