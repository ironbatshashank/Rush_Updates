Comet 3I/ATLAS Sheds Incredible "Two Kangaroos' Worth" of Dust Per Second

Comet 3I/ATLAS is making headlines, not just for being an interstellar visitor, but for its astonishing activity. IFLScience reports this cosmic wanderer is currently shedding an incredible "at least two kangaroos' worth of dust every second" into space.

Discovered in 2019, this unique comet is demonstrating an exceptionally rapid rate of material loss. Astronomers are closely observing 3I/ATLAS, as its dramatic dust expulsion offers rare insights into the composition of comets originating beyond our solar system. This intense shedding makes it a crucial object for understanding interstellar chemistry and the forces at play in deep space. Keep an eye on this fascinating, dusty spectacle!
