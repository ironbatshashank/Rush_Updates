Fatal Hong Kong Cargo Jet Crash: Two Dead as Plane Skids into Sea

HONG KONG – A tragic incident at Hong Kong International Airport (HKIA) has resulted in two fatalities after a cargo jet skidded off the runway and plunged into the sea. As reported by Bloomberg.com, the accident occurred, prompting an immediate response from emergency services who launched a recovery operation.

Authorities have commenced a thorough investigation into the devastating aviation incident. The focus will now shift to understanding the factors behind the plane's departure from the runway, highlighting critical air safety questions at one of Asia's busiest transport hubs. Updates are expected as the investigation unfolds.
