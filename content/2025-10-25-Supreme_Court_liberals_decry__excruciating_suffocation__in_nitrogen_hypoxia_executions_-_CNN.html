Supreme Court Liberals Decry "Excruciating Suffocation" in Nitrogen Hypoxia Executions

The Supreme Court's liberal wing, including Justices Sotomayor, Kagan, and Jackson, has fiercely condemned nitrogen hypoxia executions, calling the method ‘excruciating suffocation.’ In a scathing dissent, they argue the controversial death penalty method violates the Eighth Amendment’s prohibition against cruel and unusual punishment.

Their strong critique, highlighted by CNN, comes as states like Alabama have begun using nitrogen gas, leading to concerns about inmate suffering. The justices expressed alarm after the Court declined to halt recent executions, intensifying the debate over humane capital punishment and the constitutionality of this new execution method.
