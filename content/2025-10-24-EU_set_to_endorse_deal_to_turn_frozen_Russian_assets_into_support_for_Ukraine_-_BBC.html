EU set to endorse deal to turn frozen Russian assets into support for Ukraine - BBC

24 Oct 2025

EU Greenlights Landmark Deal: Frozen Russian Assets to Aid Ukraine

The European Union is on the brink of endorsing a landmark deal to transform frozen Russian central bank assets into vital financial support for Ukraine. Expected to provide billions of euros annually, these funds will primarily bolster Ukraine's defence capabilities and aid crucial reconstruction efforts.

This pivotal move, widely reported by the BBC, marks a significant shift in how the EU supports Kyiv, utilizing resources directly from the aggressor. The formal approval paves the way for a new stream of dedicated aid, reinforcing the EU's unwavering commitment to Ukraine's sovereignty and recovery. This groundbreaking decision aims to ensure sustained financial backing for a nation rebuilding amidst ongoing conflict.
