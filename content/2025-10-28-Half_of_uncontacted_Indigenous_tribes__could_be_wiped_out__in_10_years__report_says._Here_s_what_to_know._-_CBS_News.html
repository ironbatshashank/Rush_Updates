Urgent Warning: Half of Uncontacted Indigenous Tribes Could Vanish in 10 Years, Report Reveals

A chilling CBS News report highlights a dire warning: half of the world's uncontacted Indigenous tribes face potential extinction within the next decade. These incredibly vulnerable groups, primarily in the Amazon rainforest, are threatened by rampant illegal logging, mining, and the spread of diseases from encroaching outsiders.

Experts emphasize that without immediate, reinforced land protection and respect for their self-determination, invaluable cultures, unique knowledge, and significant biodiversity could be irreversibly lost. This urgent crisis demands global attention to safeguard these last bastions of uncontacted humanity before it's too late.
