Ray Dalio: America's Economy Developing 'Dependency' on Top 1%

Bridgewater Associates founder Ray Dalio warns that America is cultivating a concerning "dependency" on its top 1% of productive workers, according to a recent Fortune report. Dalio highlights a stark economic divide where the bottom 60% of the workforce are increasingly struggling and unproductive. This widening gap, he argues, poses significant risks to the nation's long-term stability and prosperity. Dalio's insights underscore the urgent need to address the challenges faced by a substantial portion of the US population to foster a more balanced and resilient economic future.
