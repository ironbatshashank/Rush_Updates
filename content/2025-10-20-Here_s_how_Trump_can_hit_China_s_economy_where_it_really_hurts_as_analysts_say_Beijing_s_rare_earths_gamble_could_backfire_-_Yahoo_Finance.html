Trump's Rare Earths Play: Hitting China Where It Hurts as Beijing's 'Gamble' Could Backfire

Former President Donald Trump could target China's economy by leveraging a surprising vulnerability: rare earth minerals. While Beijing dominates the global supply chain for these critical minerals, essential for everything from high-tech to defense, analysts suggest this very dominance could be its Achilles' heel.

Experts indicate that if a Trump administration were to impose sanctions on Chinese rare earth exports or significantly boost domestic and allied production, it could severely disrupt China's crucial industries. Beijing's perceived "gamble" of weaponizing these minerals might ultimately backfire, accelerating international efforts to diversify supplies. This move could weaken China's long-term economic leverage, hitting Beijing where it truly hurts.
