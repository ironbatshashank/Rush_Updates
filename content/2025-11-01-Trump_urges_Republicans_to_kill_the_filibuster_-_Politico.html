Trump Urges Republicans to Kill Filibuster: Reshaping Senate Power?

Former President Donald Trump is strongly urging Republicans to eliminate the Senate filibuster, a move reported by Politico. He argues this is crucial for advancing conservative legislation and avoiding gridlock if the GOP secures a future congressional majority.

Trump believes ending the 60-vote threshold would empower Republicans to swiftly pass their agenda without facing obstruction. This political strategy marks a significant pivot, given how frequently Republicans utilized the filibuster during Democratic presidencies.

The controversial proposal could dramatically reshape the dynamics of the U.S. Senate, intensify partisan battles, and accelerate legislative changes. It underscores a growing debate within the Republican Party over the foundational rules of legislative power.
