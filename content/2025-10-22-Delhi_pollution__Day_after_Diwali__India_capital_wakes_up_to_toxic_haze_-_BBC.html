Delhi Pollution Crisis: Toxic Haze Engulfs Capital Post-Diwali (BBC Report)

The day after Diwali, Delhi awoke to a thick, toxic haze, confirming concerns highlighted by the BBC. India's capital city saw its air quality plummet to "severe" and "hazardous" levels, with the Air Quality Index (AQI) soaring past 400 in many areas.

The dangerous pollution surge is attributed to a combination of widespread firecracker emissions during the festive period and persistent stubble burning in neighbouring states. Residents reported burning eyes and respiratory discomfort, underscoring the severe health risks. This annual post-Diwali crisis reiterates the urgent need for stricter enforcement and long-term strategies to tackle Delhi's escalating air pollution challenge.
