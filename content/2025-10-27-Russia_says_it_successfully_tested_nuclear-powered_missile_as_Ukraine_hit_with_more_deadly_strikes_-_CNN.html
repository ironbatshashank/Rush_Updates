Russia Confirms Nuclear-Powered Missile Test Amid Ukraine Attacks

Russia has announced the successful test of its nuclear-powered cruise missile, Burevestnik (also known as Skyfall), a development confirmed by President Vladimir Putin. This strategic military update emerges as Ukraine continues to face a barrage of deadly missile and drone strikes across its cities, intensifying the ongoing conflict.

The Burevestnik is touted for its unlimited range and ability to bypass air defense systems, marking a significant advancement in Russia's military capabilities. While Moscow frames this as a defense achievement, its timing during heightened tensions raises global security concerns. As reported by CNN, the international community is closely monitoring these developments, emphasizing the volatile geopolitical climate and potential for escalation.
