RFK Jr. Plans CDC Scrutiny of Childhood Vaccine Schedule: A Major Shift?

Presidential candidate Robert F. Kennedy Jr. has announced intentions to direct the CDC to re-evaluate the childhood vaccine schedule. This potential move, highlighted by NPR's Shots - Health News, signals a significant shift in public health policy.

If elected, Kennedy aims for a thorough review of recommended childhood vaccines and their timing. The proposal is expected to ignite considerable discussion among medical professionals, parents, and public health advocates regarding vaccine efficacy and safety protocols. This re-examination could reshape vaccination guidelines for future generations, making it a critical topic for public health discourse.
