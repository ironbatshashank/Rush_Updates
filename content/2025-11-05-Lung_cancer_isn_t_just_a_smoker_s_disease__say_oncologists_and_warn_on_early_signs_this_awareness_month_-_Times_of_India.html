Lung Cancer Awareness Month: Oncologists Warn Non-Smokers at Risk, Urge Early Detection

During Lung Cancer Awareness Month, oncologists, as highlighted by the Times of India, are debunking the dangerous myth that lung cancer is exclusively a "smoker's disease." This crucial message emphasizes that non-smokers are also susceptible due to factors like environmental pollution, genetics, and secondhand smoke exposure.

Experts urge heightened awareness of early signs. Don't dismiss persistent cough, breathlessness, chest pain, or unexplained weight loss. Early detection significantly improves treatment outcomes. This campaign aims to break stigmas and encourage proactive health checks, ensuring no one overlooks potential symptoms simply because they don't smoke. Your lungs matter – be vigilant.
