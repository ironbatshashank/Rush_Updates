Satellite Image Reveals White House Wing Demolished for Trump Ballroom Plan, BBC Reports

New satellite imagery confirms a section of the historic White House complex has been demolished, linked to a controversial plan for a grand ballroom proposed by former President Donald Trump.

The BBC reports the demolition involves a specific wing, raising questions about modifications to the iconic residence. The proposed "Trump Ballroom" project, previously undisclosed, aimed to significantly expand the executive mansion's entertainment facilities. This significant structural change, now visible from space, highlights ongoing debates surrounding presidential renovations and the preservation of national landmarks.
