Deadly Car Blast Near Red Fort Kills 8 in New Delhi, Police Investigate

A devastating car blast near New Delhi's iconic Red Fort has claimed the lives of at least eight people, according to India's police, as reported by AP News. The powerful explosion, which occurred in the vicinity of the historic landmark, has sent shockwaves across the capital.

Emergency services rushed to the scene, which has been cordoned off as authorities launch a full-scale investigation into the cause of the deadly incident. While details remain scarce, the event raises significant security concerns in the bustling metropolis. Officials are urging calm as the probe continues into this tragic Red Fort area blast.
