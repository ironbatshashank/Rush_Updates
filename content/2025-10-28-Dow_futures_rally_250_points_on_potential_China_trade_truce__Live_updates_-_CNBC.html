Dow Futures Surge: China Trade Truce Hopes Drive 250-Point Rally

Dow futures are rallying sharply, up approximately 250 points, on renewed optimism for a potential trade truce between the United States and China. Reported by CNBC as live updates unfold, the positive sentiment comes amid speculation that high-level trade talks could yield a breakthrough.

Investors are reacting positively to the prospect of de-escalation in the protracted trade war, which has been a significant source of market volatility. A successful resolution would likely boost global economic growth expectations and alleviate pressure on supply chains, signaling a hopeful outlook for the stock market. This shift reflects growing confidence that a deal might be on the horizon, easing investor concerns.
