S&P 500 Rebounds: AMD and AI Stocks Fuel Market Recovery

The S&P 500 staged a notable rebound today, erasing some losses from Tuesday's market rout. Leading the charge was chipmaker AMD, whose shares climbed significantly as investors reassessed the tech landscape.

This recovery extended to several key AI trades, signaling renewed investor confidence in the artificial intelligence sector. After recent volatility, the broader tech sector benefited, driving the overall stock market higher. Investors watched closely as the market regained momentum, particularly in high-growth areas like AI, suggesting a potential shift in sentiment heading into the week's close.
