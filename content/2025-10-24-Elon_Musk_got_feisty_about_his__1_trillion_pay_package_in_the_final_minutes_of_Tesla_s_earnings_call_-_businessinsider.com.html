Elon Musk "Feisty" Defending Tesla's Trillion-Dollar Pay Package

San Francisco, CA – Elon Musk reportedly got "feisty" in the final minutes of Tesla's recent earnings call, vehemently defending his controversial $1 trillion pay package. As detailed by Business Insider, Musk addressed the compensation plan, which was voided by a Delaware court, with characteristic vigor.

He underscored the importance of the upcoming June shareholder vote, urging investors to ratify the performance-based award. Musk emphasized that the package is designed to incentivize groundbreaking achievements for the electric vehicle giant, potentially making him the world's first trillionaire. The heated exchange highlighted the ongoing scrutiny surrounding executive compensation at Tesla and Musk's pivotal role in its future.
