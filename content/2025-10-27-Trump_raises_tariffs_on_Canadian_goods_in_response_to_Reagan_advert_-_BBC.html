Trump Slaps New Tariffs on Canadian Goods Amid Reagan Advert Controversy

(BBC) – Former US President Donald Trump has announced new tariffs on Canadian goods, citing a recent advert featuring Ronald Reagan as the catalyst. The decision escalates trade tensions between the North American neighbours, with Trump’s campaign confirming the measures are a direct retaliation to the Canadian-produced advert.

The controversial footage, using archival clips of Reagan, has been widely interpreted as indirectly critical of Trump’s political stance. While details on the specific Canadian exports targeted are emerging, the move signals a potential return to the protectionist trade policies of Trump’s previous administration. Analysts warn of significant economic repercussions, highlighting an unusual blend of political messaging and international trade disputes.
