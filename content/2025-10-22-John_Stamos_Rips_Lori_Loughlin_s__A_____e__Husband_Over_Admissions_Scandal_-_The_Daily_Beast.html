John Stamos Slams Lori Loughlin's 'A*e' Husband Over College Admissions Scandal

In a candid interview with The Daily Beast, beloved actor John Stamos didn't hold back, directly labeling Lori Loughlin’s husband, Mossimo Giannulli, an "a*e" regarding their involvement in the notorious college admissions scandal.

Stamos, long-time co-star of Loughlin on Full House and Fuller House, expressed clear sympathy for his former castmate, who served two months in prison for her role in Operation Varsity Blues. However, he reserved his harshest criticism for Giannulli, who received a five-month sentence, implying he was the primary instigator. Stamos’s comments shed light on the ongoing fallout and his strong feelings about the high-profile scandal.
