Tom Bergeron Returns to 'Dancing With The Stars' Five Years After Shocking Exit, Deadline Reports

The ballroom is buzzing! Beloved host Tom Bergeron is officially returning to "Dancing With The Stars," five years after his shocking exit, Deadline reports. Fans were devastated by his departure in 2020, following an impressive 15-year tenure as the show's charismatic frontman.

Now, Bergeron's highly anticipated comeback promises to reignite the magic. His signature wit and warm presence are expected to once again captivate audiences, making this one of the biggest DWTS announcements in years. Get ready to cha-cha-cha with Tom Bergeron back on the dance floor!
