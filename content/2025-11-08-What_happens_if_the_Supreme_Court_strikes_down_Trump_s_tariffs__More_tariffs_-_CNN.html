08 Nov 2025

Supreme Court Ruling on Trump Tariffs: Why More Import Taxes May Be Next, Per CNN

A potential Supreme Court decision striking down former President Trump's tariffs could paradoxically lead to more trade barriers, CNN reports.

Many of Trump's import taxes, particularly those on steel and aluminum, relied on Section 232 of the Trade Expansion Act, a national security claim. If SCOTUS limits presidential authority in this area, it empowers Congress to legislate on trade policy.

This shift doesn't guarantee tariff removal; rather, it could prompt lawmakers to impose their own new duties or reform existing agreements. The outcome could usher in a new era of unpredictable U.S. trade policy, impacting businesses and consumers with potentially different, but still present, import costs. The battle over who controls America's trade future is far from over.
