Interstellar object 3I/ATLAS is about to get very active — Space photo of the week - Live Science

27 Oct 2025

An extraordinary visitor from beyond our solar system is gearing up for a celestial spectacle! Interstellar object 3I/ATLAS, the first interstellar comet ever discovered, is predicted to become "very active," according to Live Science.

Astronomers anticipate a dramatic outburst as this unique object approaches the Sun, likely due to volatile materials rapidly sublimating. This activity could cause 3I/ATLAS to brighten significantly, offering a rare and crucial opportunity to study pristine material from another star system up close.

Designated as Live Science's 'Space Photo of the Week,' its potential for stunning visuals and groundbreaking scientific data is immense. Get ready to witness a truly unique cosmic event unfold!
