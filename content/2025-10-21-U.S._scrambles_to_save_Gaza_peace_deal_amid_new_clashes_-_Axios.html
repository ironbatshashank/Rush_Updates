U.S. Scrambles to Save Gaza Peace Deal Amid Renewed Clashes, Axios Reports

The United States is reportedly in a frantic diplomatic push to prevent the collapse of a potential Gaza peace deal, as new clashes threaten to unravel delicate negotiations. According to Axios, the Biden administration is intensifying efforts to secure a lasting ceasefire and a hostage release agreement amidst a deteriorating security situation.

U.S. officials are deeply concerned that escalating violence could derail months of diplomatic work. The urgency underscores the critical need for a breakthrough to address the humanitarian crisis in Gaza and restore regional stability. International pressure is mounting for all parties to commit to de-escalation and revive peace talks.
