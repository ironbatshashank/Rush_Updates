‘When Women Thrive, the Community Thrives’: Inside Variety’s Power of Women Event Featuring Jamie Lee Curtis, Sydney Sweeney, Kate Hudson, Nicole Scherzinger and Wanda Sykes - Variety

31 Oct 2025

Variety’s recent Power of Women event brilliantly highlighted the inspiring theme: "When Women Thrive, the Community Thrives." The star-studded gathering celebrated incredible achievements, featuring Jamie Lee Curtis, Sydney Sweeney, Kate Hudson, Nicole Scherzinger, and Wanda Sykes as honorees.

Each celebrated woman shared powerful insights into their philanthropic efforts and the profound impact women have on society. The event underscored how empowering women directly uplifts communities, fostering positive change and progress across all sectors. This powerful Variety event served as a potent reminder of the collective strength and profound impact women have in shaping a brighter future for all.
