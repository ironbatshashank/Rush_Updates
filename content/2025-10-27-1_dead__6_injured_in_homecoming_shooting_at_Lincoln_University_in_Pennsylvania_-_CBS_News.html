Fatal Shooting Mars Lincoln University Homecoming: 1 Dead, 6 Injured in PA Tragedy

LINCOLN UNIVERSITY, PA – A tragic shooting incident during Lincoln University’s annual homecoming celebrations has left one person dead and six others injured. The devastating event occurred in the early hours at the Pennsylvania campus, sending shockwaves through the community.

Pennsylvania State Police are actively investigating the circumstances surrounding the fatal shooting. Authorities quickly responded to the scene, and officials are working to identify suspects and determine a motive.

The university has expressed profound sorrow, offering support and resources to students and staff grappling with the loss and trauma. This somber incident raises urgent concerns about safety at campus events. Further details will be released as the ongoing investigation progresses.
