Ramsey Naito Departs as Paramount Animation President

Paramount Animation President Ramsey Naito is reportedly stepping down from her role, marking a significant leadership change for the studio. The news, first broken by Deadline, signals a high-profile exit in the animation industry.

Naito, who took the helm in 2020, oversaw the development and production of several key animated features for Paramount. Her tenure included projects like the critically acclaimed Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles: Mutant Mayhem and The SpongeBob Movie: Sponge on the Run. This departure leaves a void at the top of Paramount's animation division as the studio looks to chart its future course. A search for a new president is expected to commence soon.
