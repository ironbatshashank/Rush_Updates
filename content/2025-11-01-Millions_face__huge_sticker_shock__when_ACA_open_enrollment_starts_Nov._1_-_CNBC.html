ACA Open Enrollment: Millions Face Sticker Shock as Premiums Rise Nov. 1

Millions of Americans are bracing for significant "sticker shock" as ACA Open Enrollment begins on November 1st. CNBC reports that many will find health insurance premiums are substantially higher this year, largely due to expiring enhanced federal subsidies and increasing healthcare costs.

This could lead to unexpectedly expensive plans for those purchasing coverage through the Affordable Care Act marketplace. Experts urge consumers to thoroughly compare available plans and check eligibility for financial assistance at Healthcare.gov. Don't wait; securing affordable coverage requires careful review this enrollment season.
