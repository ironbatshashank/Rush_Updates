UConn Women's Basketball Opener Relocated: Germany Game Moves to U.S. Naval Academy

Big news for UConn women's basketball fans! The Huskies' highly anticipated regular season opener, originally planned for Germany, has been officially moved. According to CT Insider, the game will now be played at the historic U.S. Naval Academy in Annapolis, Maryland.

This significant schedule change means UConn will no longer face a German Bundesliga team abroad. Instead, fans can catch the UConn women's basketball team kicking off their season stateside. The move to Annapolis, Maryland, offers a unique and patriotic venue for the opening tip-off. While the international trip is off, the excitement for UConn basketball's start remains high as they prepare to launch their new season at a memorable U.S. location.
