Epstein Files: Trump Signs Bill, Public Awaits Release Timeline

President Trump has signed legislation mandating the expedited release of previously sealed court documents related to convicted sex offender Jeffrey Epstein.

With the bill now law, attention immediately shifts to when these highly anticipated files will become public. Expected to shed new light on Epstein's vast sex trafficking network and potential accomplices, the move follows years of public demand and calls for transparency.

While a crucial step towards answers for victims and ending speculation, the exact release timeline and extent of revelations remain the paramount focus for the public and media alike, as reported by ABC News.
