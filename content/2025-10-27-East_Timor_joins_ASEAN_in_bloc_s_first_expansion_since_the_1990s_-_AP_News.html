East Timor Joins ASEAN: Southeast Asian Bloc's First Expansion Since 1990s

East Timor (Timor-Leste) has officially become the 11th member of the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN), marking the regional bloc's first expansion since the 1990s. This significant development, widely reported by AP News, fulfills a long-held aspiration for the young nation.

The decision signifies a pivotal moment for both East Timor and Southeast Asia. Full ASEAN membership promises enhanced economic cooperation, increased trade, and greater diplomatic influence for Timor-Leste. For ASEAN, it strengthens the bloc's collective presence and voice, fostering broader regional integration and stability. This expansion underscores the dynamic evolution of the vital Southeast Asian partnership.
