PayPal Powers ChatGPT: First Payments Wallet Arrives via OpenAI Deal

In a landmark announcement reported by CNBC, PayPal has signed a pivotal deal with OpenAI, making it the first payments wallet directly integrated into ChatGPT. This groundbreaking partnership allows users to complete secure online transactions and purchases seamlessly within the AI chat interface.

The integration signifies a major leap for AI commerce, enabling direct e-commerce experiences powered by PayPal's trusted platform. For businesses, this opens new avenues for sales directly through conversational AI. This move positions PayPal at the forefront of the evolving digital payments landscape, merging convenience with artificial intelligence.
