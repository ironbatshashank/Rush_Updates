Afghanistan's Historic Blue Mosque Damaged by Earthquake, DW Reports

Mazar-i-Sharif, Afghanistan – Afghanistan's iconic Blue Mosque, officially known as the Shrine of Hazrat Ali, has suffered significant damage following a series of recent powerful earthquakes. DW reports widespread concern as sections of the ancient structure, famed for its dazzling turquoise tiles and intricate mosaics, show cracks and fallen masonry.

This revered site, a central pilgrimage destination and a symbol of Afghan cultural and religious heritage, faces an uncertain future. Experts are assessing the full extent of the damage to the historic landmark in Mazar-i-Sharif. Efforts to preserve this irreplaceable monument are now paramount, highlighting the fragility of ancient architecture against natural disasters.
