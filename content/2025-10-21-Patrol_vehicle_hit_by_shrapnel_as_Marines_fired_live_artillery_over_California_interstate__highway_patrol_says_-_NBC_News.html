Shrapnel Hits Patrol Car as Marines Fire Live Artillery Over California Interstate

A U.S. Marine Corps live-fire artillery exercise took an unexpected turn when shrapnel struck a California Highway Patrol (CHP) vehicle traveling over a California interstate. According to NBC News, the incident occurred during a routine live-fire training session where artillery rounds were fired over the major thoroughfare.

The patrol vehicle sustained minor damage, but fortunately, no injuries were reported to the officer inside. This close call has prompted an investigation by the Marine Corps to determine the cause and prevent future occurrences. The incident highlights significant military exercise safety concerns when operations are conducted near public highways and civilian traffic in California.
