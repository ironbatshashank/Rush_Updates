Hungary's Mol Floats Strategic Shift from Russian Oil Ahead of Orban-Trump Talks

Hungary's energy giant, Mol, is reportedly exploring strategies to significantly reduce its reliance on Russian oil. This pivotal development, highlighted by Bloomberg, signals a major shift in Hungary's energy policy amidst evolving geopolitical landscapes.

The move comes strategically ahead of Prime Minister Viktor Orban's anticipated meeting with former US President Donald Trump. Historically dependent on Russian energy, Mol's potential diversification reflects a broader push for enhanced energy security and independence. This strategic pivot could reshape Hungary's standing within Europe and impact future US-Hungary relations. Analysts suggest oil supply and geopolitical alliances will be key discussion points during the high-profile Orban-Trump talks.
