24 Oct 2025

Major SNAP Benefit Cuts Hit 25+ States This November

Families relying on food assistance face a significant challenge as over 25 states are set to cut SNAP benefits, specifically ending emergency allotments, starting in November, Politico reports.

This move, affecting millions of low-income households, marks the end of temporary COVID-19 provisions that provided additional monthly support. Many could see their SNAP assistance reduced by at least $95, impacting their ability to afford groceries. The cuts reflect a shift away from pandemic-era aid as states adjust budgets. Recipients are urged to check their state’s specific guidelines and explore local food assistance resources for continued support during this transition.
