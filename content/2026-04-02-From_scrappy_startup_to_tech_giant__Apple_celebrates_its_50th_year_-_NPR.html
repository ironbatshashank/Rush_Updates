Apple Marks 50 Years: From Scrappy Startup to Tech Giant

Cupertino, CA – What began in a garage now dominates the global tech landscape. Apple is proudly celebrating its 50th anniversary, a remarkable journey from humble beginnings to an unparalleled tech giant.

From the ambitious vision of Steve Jobs and Steve Wozniak in 1976, Apple Inc. has redefined innovation. Its relentless pursuit of excellence transformed personal computing with the Mac, revolutionized communication with the iPhone, and shaped countless industries. As reported by NPR, this golden jubilee solidifies Apple's legacy as a pivotal force in modern technology. The company continues to push boundaries, shaping our digital future globally.
