Reinier de Ridder loses to Brendan Allen at UFC Fight Night in Canada - Al Jazeera

20 Oct 2025

Al Jazeera reports a significant upset at UFC Fight Night in Canada as Reinier de Ridder, the dominant ONE Championship star, suffered a second-round submission loss to Brendan Allen. The highly anticipated middleweight bout saw Allen, known for his relentless grappling, secure a rear-naked choke against 'The Dutch Knight.' This marks a rare defeat for de Ridder, who ventured into the Octagon for this special cross-promotional contest. Allen’s impressive victory, delivered before a fervent Canadian audience, solidifies his position as a formidable contender in the competitive UFC middleweight division, sending ripples across the global MMA landscape.
