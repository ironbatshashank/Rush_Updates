Casey Means' Surgeon General Hearing: What NPR Is Watching

As Dr. Casey Means, the President's nominee for Surgeon General, faces her confirmation hearing, NPR listeners will be keenly watching several key areas. Known for her advocacy of functional medicine and metabolic health, senators will likely scrutinize how her "root cause" approach aligns with traditional public health strategies.

Expect questions regarding her vision for addressing chronic disease, diet, and lifestyle interventions on a national scale. Her background as co-founder of Levels Health, a continuous glucose monitoring company, may also come under the spotlight. The hearing will reveal if her unconventional perspective can garner bipartisan support to bridge the gap between preventative care and mainstream medicine, shaping the future of America's health agenda.
