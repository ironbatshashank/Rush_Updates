Trump administration finalizes plan to open pristine Alaska wildlife refuge to oil and gas drilling - NPR

25 Oct 2025

The Trump administration has finalized a controversial plan to open a vast portion of the pristine Arctic National Wildlife Refuge (ANWR) in Alaska for oil and gas drilling. This landmark decision, reported by NPR, ends decades of protection for a region considered one of America's last great wild places.

Environmental groups swiftly condemned the move, citing irreparable damage to delicate ecosystems and native wildlife, including polar bears and caribou. Supporters, however, argue the drilling is crucial for U.S. energy independence and job creation. The finalization paves the way for future lease sales, setting the stage for significant energy development in the Arctic.
