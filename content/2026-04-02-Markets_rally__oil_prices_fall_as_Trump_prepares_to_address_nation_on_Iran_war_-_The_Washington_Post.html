Markets Surge, Oil Plummets Ahead of Trump's Crucial Iran Address

(The Washington Post) Global markets rallied significantly today, with stocks soaring and oil prices plummeting, as investors brace for President Donald Trump's highly anticipated address to the nation on escalating tensions with Iran. This market shift signals cautious optimism for de-escalation following initial reports suggesting a more measured response from both sides.

Brent crude futures dropped over 4%, indicating reduced fears of widespread supply disruptions in the Middle East. All eyes are now on the White House, where Trump is expected to outline the U.S. strategy, a pivotal moment that could determine the course of potential conflict. The Washington Post will provide live coverage of this critical address.
