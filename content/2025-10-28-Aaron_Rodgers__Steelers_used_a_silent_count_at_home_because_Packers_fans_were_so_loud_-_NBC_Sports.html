Aaron Rodgers: Steelers Used Silent Count at Home Due to Roaring Packers Fans

NFL quarterback Aaron Rodgers recently revealed a surprising detail about a past game against the Pittsburgh Steelers. According to Rodgers, the Steelers were forced to use a silent count at home in Acrisure Stadium. The reason? Visiting Green Bay Packers fans were so incredibly loud, they effectively created a hostile road environment for Pittsburgh.

This observation, reported by NBC Sports, underscores the immense dedication and travel power of the Packers faithful. It's rare for an NFL team to need a silent count in their own stadium, highlighting the remarkable noise generated by Packers fans even in Pittsburgh. Rodgers’ comment emphasizes the unique passion that follows the Packers nationwide.
