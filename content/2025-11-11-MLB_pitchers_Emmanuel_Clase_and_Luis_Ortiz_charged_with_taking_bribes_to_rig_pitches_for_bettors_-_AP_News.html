MLB Pitchers Emmanuel Clase, Luis Ortiz Charged in Bribery, Pitch-Rigging Scandal

Major League Baseball is reeling today following reports that star pitchers Emmanuel Clase and Luis Ortiz have been charged with accepting bribes to rig pitches for sports bettors.

According to AP News, Clase, the Cleveland Guardians closer, and Ortiz, a Pittsburgh Pirates pitcher, allegedly manipulated game outcomes—a bombshell revelation rocking the integrity of the sport. This high-profile bribery scandal highlights growing concerns about sports betting's influence on professional sports. Both players face severe penalties from MLB and potential legal consequences as the investigation unfolds, sending shockwaves through the league and its fanbase.
