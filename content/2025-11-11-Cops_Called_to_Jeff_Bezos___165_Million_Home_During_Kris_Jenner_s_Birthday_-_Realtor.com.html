Cops Called to Jeff Bezos' $165 Million Home During Kris Jenner's Birthday

An unusual incident unfolded at Jeff Bezos' opulent $165 million Beverly Hills estate recently, as police were called to the property. The unexpected response, first reported by Realtor.com, surprisingly coincided with reality TV matriarch Kris Jenner's birthday celebrations.

Details regarding the nature of the disturbance at the Amazon founder's sprawling mansion remain scarce. Authorities confirmed that no arrests were made, and no crime was reported in connection with the call. While the exact reason for police intervention is unknown, this unexpected event adds a touch of Hollywood mystery to the typically private world of the billionaire and his luxurious home.
