Mark Andrews: With Lamar Jackson Back, Baltimore Ravens Are "Dangerous Team"

Baltimore Ravens tight end Mark Andrews has declared the team a "dangerous" force with quarterback Lamar Jackson's return, according to NBC Sports. Andrews emphasized the significant impact Jackson's dynamic playmaking has on the entire Ravens offense.

After missing the end of last season due to injury, Jackson's healthy presence reignites Baltimore's championship aspirations. This strong vote of confidence from a key offensive leader like Andrews underscores the team's belief that a fully healthy Lamar Jackson makes the Ravens an immediate threat in the NFL and a formidable opponent for any team in the AFC.
