Sarkozy Freed After 20 Days: French Conspiracy Probe Continues

Former French President Nicolas Sarkozy has been released from jail after just 20 days. His swift detention stemmed from a long-running investigation into alleged illicit Libyan funding for his 2007 presidential campaign.

The high-profile conspiracy probe involves accusations of corruption, illegal campaign financing, and concealment of public funds. Sarkozy, who vehemently denies all wrongdoing, remains under judicial scrutiny as the complex case unfolds.

While freed, the investigation is far from over, with legal proceedings expected to continue. This development keeps the legal saga surrounding the ex-president firmly in the national and international spotlight, highlighting the ongoing battle against high-level corruption in France.
