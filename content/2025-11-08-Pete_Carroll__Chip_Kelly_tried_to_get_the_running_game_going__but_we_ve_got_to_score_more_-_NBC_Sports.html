Pete Carroll Praises Chip Kelly's Run Game Efforts, Emphasizes Need for More Scoring for Seahawks

Seattle Seahawks coach Pete Carroll recently weighed in on offensive coordinator Chip Kelly's impact, as reported by NBC Sports. Carroll acknowledged Kelly's dedicated efforts to establish a strong running game, stating, "Chip Kelly tried to get the running game going."

However, Carroll quickly pivoted to the ultimate goal, emphasizing the critical need for the Seahawks to "score more." This statement underscores the team's ongoing challenge to convert offensive drives and ground gains into crucial points on the scoreboard. While the run game shows promise under Kelly, Carroll's comments highlight a clear objective: increased offensive efficiency to secure victories.
