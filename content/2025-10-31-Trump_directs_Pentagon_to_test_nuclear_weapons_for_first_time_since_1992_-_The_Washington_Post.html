Trump Orders Nuclear Weapons Test Planning, First Since 1992 – Washington Post Exclusive

Former President Donald Trump reportedly directed the Pentagon to prepare for a nuclear weapons test, an unprecedented move since 1992, according to a Washington Post investigation. This directive, if executed, would break a three-decade moratorium on U.S. full-scale nuclear testing. Sources indicate the intent was to demonstrate U.S. resolve and potentially pressure adversaries like Russia and China regarding arms control. Experts warn such a test could ignite a dangerous global arms race, destabilizing international relations and potentially undermining non-proliferation efforts. The news sparks significant debate over its strategic necessity and global ramifications for national security.
