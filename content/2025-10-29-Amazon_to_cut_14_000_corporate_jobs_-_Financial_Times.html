Amazon to Cut 14,000 Corporate Jobs Amid Economic Headwinds, Reports FT

Amazon is set to eliminate 14,000 corporate jobs, according to the Financial Times, marking a significant wave of Amazon layoffs impacting its global workforce. These job cuts come as the tech giant navigates a challenging economic landscape and corrects for over-hiring during the pandemic-driven surge in online demand.

The reductions, first announced by CEO Andy Jassy, will primarily affect departments like Devices, Books, and People, Experience, and Technology (PXT). This move highlights a broader trend of cost-cutting across the tech sector, as companies brace for potential recession and adjust to slower growth. The 14,000 jobs represent a substantial reduction, emphasizing Amazon's strategic shift in response to current economic realities.
