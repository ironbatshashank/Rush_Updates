Tragic Loss: 'Fresh Prince' Child Actor Floyd Roger Myers Jr. Dies at 42

Hollywood mourns the passing of Floyd Roger Myers Jr., the beloved child actor known for his memorable role in the iconic sitcom ‘The Fresh Prince of Bel-Air.’ Myers Jr. has sadly died at the age of 42.

While details surrounding his death remain limited, the news has sent shockwaves through fans and the entertainment industry. Myers Jr. notably appeared as a young "Little Will" in the fan-favorite "B-Ball Curfew" episode during the show's fourth season, leaving a lasting impression. His passing marks a poignant loss, reminding many of his significant, albeit brief, contribution to a classic TV series. Fans are sharing tributes across social media for the late 'Fresh Prince' actor.
