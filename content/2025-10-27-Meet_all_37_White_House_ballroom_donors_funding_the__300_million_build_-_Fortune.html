Fortune Uncovers All 37 Donors Behind $300M White House Build

Fortune has unveiled the identities of all 37 private donors who collectively funded the monumental $300 million White House build. This unprecedented philanthropic effort, detailed in a new report, sheds light on the individuals and foundations behind the significant enhancements to America's most iconic residence.

Historically, such major private contributions to White House projects have often remained confidential. The newly revealed list includes a mix of prominent business leaders and influential philanthropists, whose generosity underpins a massive infrastructure upgrade.

Their collective investment ensures the modernization and preservation of critical White House spaces, including its famed ballroom, for future generations. Fortune’s report offers a rare, transparent look into this exclusive circle funding a key piece of national heritage.
