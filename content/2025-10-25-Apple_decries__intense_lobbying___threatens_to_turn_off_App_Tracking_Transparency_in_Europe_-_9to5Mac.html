Apple Threatens ATT Halt in Europe Amid Intense Lobbying

Apple is reportedly considering disabling its crucial App Tracking Transparency (ATT) feature in Europe. According to 9to5Mac, the tech giant is decrying "intense lobbying" from rivals aiming to weaken privacy protections within the Digital Services Act (DSA).

Apple warns that if forced to allow third-party app stores (sideloading) without robust safeguards, it might remove ATT. The company claims it cannot guarantee user privacy under such conditions, potentially exposing European users to unwanted tracking. This move signals a significant escalation in the ongoing battle over digital privacy, market control, and the future of the App Store in the EU.
