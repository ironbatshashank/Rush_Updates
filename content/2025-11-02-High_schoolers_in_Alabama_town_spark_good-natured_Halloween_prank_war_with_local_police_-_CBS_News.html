Alabama High Schoolers Spark Good-Natured Halloween Prank War with Local Police, Reports CBS News

A hilarious and good-natured Halloween prank war has erupted in an Alabama town, pitting high school students against their local police department, CBS News reports. What started as lighthearted mischief from the teens, including harmless decorations and playful "ambushes," has quickly evolved into a spirited back-and-forth rivalry.

Local police officers are embracing the challenge, responding with their own creative and funny stunts. This wholesome community event is bringing smiles and laughter to residents, proving that a little friendly competition can foster great local spirit. It's a memorable way for Alabama high schoolers and police to celebrate the Halloween season together!
