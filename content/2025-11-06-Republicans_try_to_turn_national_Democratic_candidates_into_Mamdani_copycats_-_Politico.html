GOP Tactics: Turning National Democrats Into 'Mamdani Copycats' on Abortion

A new Politico report reveals Republicans are employing a strategic tactic: branding national Democratic candidates as "Mamdani copycats." This refers to a controversial internal memo suggesting Democrats focus on state-level abortion rights post-Roe, rather than solely federal protection.

The GOP aims to pigeonhole Democrats into a state-only abortion debate, potentially diminishing their calls for national codification of abortion access. This shrewd political maneuver challenges Democratic messaging ahead of the 2024 elections, attempting to sow division and reshape the pivotal abortion rights narrative, forcing Democrats to defend their approach on this critical issue.
