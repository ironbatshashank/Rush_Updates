Trump, Vance Not Invited to Dick Cheney Funeral as All Four Living Former VPs Attend, CNN Reports

Dick Cheney's upcoming funeral services are stirring headlines, as CNN reports an exclusive guest list. Notably, former President Donald Trump and Vance will not be among those attending.

However, all four living former Vice Presidents – Mike Pence, Al Gore, Dan Quayle, and current President Joe Biden (who served as VP under Obama) – are confirmed guests. This poignant gathering highlights Cheney's distinguished legacy, bringing together a unique assembly of past U.S. leaders. The exclusions from the high-profile event underscore the carefully curated nature of the invitations, drawing a clear line on political presence at the somber occasion.
