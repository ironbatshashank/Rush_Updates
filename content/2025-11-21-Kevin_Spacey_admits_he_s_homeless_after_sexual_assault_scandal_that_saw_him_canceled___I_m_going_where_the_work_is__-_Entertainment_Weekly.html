Kevin Spacey Reveals Homelessness Amid Sexual Assault Scandal After Career Cancellation

Award-winning actor Kevin Spacey has admitted he is now homeless following his highly publicized sexual assault scandal that effectively ended his mainstream career. In a recent interview with Entertainment Weekly, Spacey stated, "I'm going where the work is," highlighting his struggle to find acting roles.

The revelation comes years after multiple allegations led to his cancellation from major projects, including House of Cards. Despite recent legal victories, the actor's professional landscape remains challenging. Spacey's future in Hollywood remains uncertain as he navigates personal hardship while seeking to rebuild his professional life.
