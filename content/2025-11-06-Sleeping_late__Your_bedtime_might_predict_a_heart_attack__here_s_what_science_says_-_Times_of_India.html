Times of India: Is Your Late Bedtime a Heart Attack Predictor? New Science Reveals Link

Ever wondered if your late-night habits impact more than just your morning energy? A groundbreaking new study, highlighted by Times of India, suggests a surprising connection: habitually sleeping late might significantly increase your risk of a heart attack.

Science indicates that consistent late bedtimes can disrupt your body’s natural circadian rhythm. This misalignment isn't just about feeling groggy; it can negatively impact cardiovascular health, potentially acting as a predictor for serious heart conditions. Researchers emphasize that maintaining a regular sleep schedule is crucial for overall well-being. So, next time you push bedtime, consider what science says about protecting your heart.
