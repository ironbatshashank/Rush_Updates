Trump Threatens NYC Federal Funding If Mamdani Wins Mayoral Race, France 24 Reports

New York City – Donald Trump has warned he would sever federal funding to New York City should Zohran Mamdani be elected mayor, according to a report from France 24. This unprecedented threat targets a major U.S. city's financial stability, injecting a contentious national political element into the upcoming municipal election.

Mamdani, a progressive candidate, has advocated for policies Trump opposes, prompting the former president's strong declaration. New York City heavily relies on federal aid for infrastructure, social services, and public safety. Trump's intervention highlights the escalating political stakes and potential economic repercussions for NYC residents should his threat materialize.
