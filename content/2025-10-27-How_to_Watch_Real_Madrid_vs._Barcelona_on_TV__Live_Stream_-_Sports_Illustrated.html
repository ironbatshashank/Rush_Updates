How to Watch Real Madrid vs. Barcelona: TV & Live Stream for El Clásico

Gear up for the most anticipated match in football! Sports Illustrated guides you on how to catch the iconic El Clásico clash between giants Real Madrid and Barcelona. This global spectacle promises breathtaking action and fierce rivalry.

In the United States, fans can exclusively live stream every thrilling moment of LaLiga, including El Clásico, on ESPN+. A subscription unlocks access to all matches, ensuring you don't miss a single play. For traditional TV viewers, select El Clásico matches may also air on ESPN Deportes. Check your local listings or streaming TV providers (like Hulu + Live TV, Sling TV, YouTube TV) that carry ESPN channels for broadcast specifics.
