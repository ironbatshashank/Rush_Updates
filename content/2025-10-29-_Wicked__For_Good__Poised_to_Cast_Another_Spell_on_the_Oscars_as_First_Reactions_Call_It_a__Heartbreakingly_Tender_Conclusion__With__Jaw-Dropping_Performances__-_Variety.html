‘Wicked: For Good’ Brews Oscar Buzz with ‘Heartbreaking’ First Reactions

“Wicked: For Good” is already enchanting critics, sparking significant Oscar buzz months ahead of its release. Variety reports initial reactions hail the highly anticipated film as a "heartbreakingly tender conclusion," highlighting "jaw-dropping performances."

This glowing early praise suggests the second part of Jon M. Chu's musical epic, starring Cynthia Erivo as Elphaba and Ariana Grande as Glinda, is poised to cast another spell, this time on Academy Award voters. The film’s powerful narrative and incredible acting are building undeniable momentum. Get ready for a cinematic experience that promises to be both unforgettable and a serious contender in the upcoming awards season. The magic is definitely back!
