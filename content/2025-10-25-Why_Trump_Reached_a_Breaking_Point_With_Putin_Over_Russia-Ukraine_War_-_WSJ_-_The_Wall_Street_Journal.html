WSJ Reveals: Trump's "Breaking Point" with Putin Over Russia-Ukraine War

The Wall Street Journal reports that Donald Trump has reached a "breaking point" with Vladimir Putin regarding the protracted Russia-Ukraine War. This significant shift comes after Trump, who previously often expressed a desire for warmer U.S.-Russia relations, has grown increasingly frustrated.

According to the WSJ, Putin's perceived intransigence and unwillingness to negotiate a swift end to the conflict have deeply challenged Trump's desire for a quick resolution. This alleged pivot suggests a tougher stance from the former President, potentially redefining his future approach to global security and the ongoing geopolitical crisis. The evolving dynamic between Trump and Putin signals a notable turn in their complex relationship amidst the devastating Ukraine war.
