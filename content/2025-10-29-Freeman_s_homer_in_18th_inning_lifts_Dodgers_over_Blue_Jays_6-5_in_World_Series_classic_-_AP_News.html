Freeman's 18th-Inning Blast Lifts Dodgers in World Series Classic

Freddie Freeman delivered an unforgettable walk-off moment, launching an 18th-inning solo home run to propel the Los Angeles Dodgers to a thrilling 6-5 victory over the Toronto Blue Jays. This marathon World Series contest, an instant classic, stretched deep into the night, keeping baseball fans on the edge of their seats.

Freeman's dramatic blast capped an epic battle of endurance and skill, securing a pivotal win for the Dodgers in the ultimate baseball showdown. The 6-5 final score reflected the nail-biting intensity of this historic MLB game, ensuring it will be etched into World Series lore.
