Mexico Lime Growers' President Murdered After Cartel Extortion Complaints

The president of a lime growers association in Michoacán, Mexico, has been found killed in his car, according to reports from CBS News. This tragic discovery comes shortly after the victim reportedly voiced concerns about escalating cartel extortion targeting the region's vital citrus industry.

The incident highlights the intensifying grip of organized crime on Mexico's agricultural sector. Drug cartels frequently demand protection payments from farmers and distributors, driving up costs and threatening livelihoods. Authorities have launched an investigation into the murder, which sends a chilling message to growers already operating under immense pressure. Fear remains high among local lime producers as they grapple with ongoing violence and economic instability.
