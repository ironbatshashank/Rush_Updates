Hurricane Melissa: Caribbean Braces for Rapid Intensification into Major Storm

Urgent warnings are being issued as Tropical Storm Melissa is now expected to rapidly intensify into a major hurricane in the Caribbean Sea. According to NPR, this quick strengthening poses a significant threat to islands in its path.

Forecasters project Melissa to become a powerful, dangerous storm within the next 24-48 hours, bringing extreme winds and heavy rainfall. Residents across the Caribbean are strongly urged to closely monitor official advisories, finalize emergency preparations, and stay informed. This rapid intensification highlights the critical need for immediate action as Melissa transforms into a formidable major hurricane.
